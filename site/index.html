Model assumptions +

A small computational evo-devo model, not a molecular or botanically calibrated simulator. Four quantitative loci are independently assorted into haploid gametes, then paired into a diploid offspring. Conspecific neighbours may contribute pollen; otherwise selfing occurs. Mutation is stochastic and rare. Resource-dependent organ growth creates phenotypic plasticity.
