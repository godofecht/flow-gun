03THE SYSTEM BENEATH

Complexity from
simple rules.

Each cell receives nutrients, moisture, and sunlight. Neighbours compete for space. Plants grow, reproduce, shade, wither, and return their resources to the earth.

From just these local interactions, the landscape assembles its own mosaics and borders. The garden has no prescribed final appearance.
